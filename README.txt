FILES
- index.html: responsive storefront prototype
- assets/: your uploaded logo and 7 product photos

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. Use the product filters, add products to the bag, test quantities and fill the checkout/enquiry form.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE
This is a front-end prototype, not yet a live e-commerce service. It currently does not send orders to the business, persist them in an admin system, or collect payments. The checkout/enquiry buttons prepare draft text to copy and share manually.

To launch a real store, configure:
1. Final selling prices for every product, including GST treatment if applicable.
2. Business WhatsApp/contact number and contact links.
3. Secure order storage/backend and customer order confirmations.
4. Payment gateway (for example, Razorpay or another supported provider) with merchant verification; never collect card/UPI credentials directly in this page.
5. Courier/shipping provider, PIN-code serviceability, shipping rates, dispatch estimates, returns/cancellations and privacy policy.
6. Domain name and HTTPS hosting.

The prices are intentionally not invented; the catalogue says "Price to be confirmed" until the seller supplies final prices. COD is a request only and must be confirmed. Online payment is shown as a future payment option but is not active.
